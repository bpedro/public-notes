Here's a list of interesting things that have been published this month (or at the very end of August):

- The [AWS Agent Registry](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/manage-agents-tools-and-skills-at-scale-with-aws-agent-registry/), announced on August 31, 2026, is "a single, governed catalog to register, discover, and manage AI agents, tools, and capabilities."
- HashiCorp's [Vault agentic IAM](https://www.hashicorp.com/en/blog/hashicorp-vault-agentic-iam-is-now-generally-available), announced on September 1, 2026, "brings agent identity, authentication, and authorization into a single operational model."
- Kong [AI Gateway 2.0](https://konghq.com/blog/product-releases/kong-ai-gateway-2-0-ga), announced on September 1, 2026, ships things like "MCP Server Bundling for governed agent tool access; dynamic modality-aware cost management; expanded model and provider coverage across Kimi, Microsoft Foundry, and Amazon SageMaker; identity-aware AI policies powered by Kong Identity; and native IAM authentication for Amazon Bedrock AgentCore."
- Solo.io's [Agentdeskop](https://www.solo.io/blog/introducing-agentdesktop), announced on September 3, 2026, is "an open-source AI tool visibility and management layer."
-  [One Tool, three surfaces](https://tyk.io/blog/one-tool-three-surfaces-from-an-openapi-file-to-an-mcp-server-without-writing-it-twice/), published on September 8, 2026, shows how you can use the Tyk AI Studio to create different interfaces for your API without ever touching its OpenAPI document.
- [Mistral raised €3B in a Series D round](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-makes-sovereign-open-weight-ai-to-frontier/), published on September 8, 2026, shows how the investment will "significantly expand Mistral's frontier research," and allow the company to accelerate its commercial growth and international footprint.
- [Five Ways to Use OpenTelemetry Beyond Observability](https://speedscale.com/blog/opentelemetry-beyond-observability/), published on September 11, 2026, shows how you can use OTel as a general-purpose data pipeline, to treat entity events as inventory, use the baggage header for routing, use metrics as a metering feed, and use spans as a payload store.
- [Arazzo is solving the right problem for AI tools](https://bump.sh/blog/arazzo-is-solving-the-right-problem-for-ai-tools-the-spec-just-isnt-there-yet/), published on September 14, 2026, showcases Bump's [Flower](https://github.com/bump-sh/flower-spec) specification, an alternative to Arazzo.
