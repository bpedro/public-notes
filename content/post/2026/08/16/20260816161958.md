> Several major AI models now watermark their output, but coverage varies widely by provider and by content type. As of August 2026, [Claude](https://claude.ai) is the clearest confirmed example of an embedded, machine-readable text watermark applied with no opt-out. Google's SynthID also watermarks generated text inside [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com). Most other providers mark images and audio today, and their text-watermarking status is either newer or not publicly confirmed.
