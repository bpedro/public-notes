> Data contracts are a way to describe the contents and guarantees of datasets. They apply practices from API design to data by treating them as data-providing services - such as data products - whose interfaces we want to define as clearly as possible. This approach significantly increases the robustness of large-scale data landscapes, and gives both people and AI agents the explicit, trustworthy metadata they need to work with data autonomously, which is why more and more enterprises are adopting them.
