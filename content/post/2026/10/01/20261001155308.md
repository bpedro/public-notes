> A Design Partner is a co-developer who first provides feedback, data, workflow access, and real-world testing in exchange for early access, preferential terms, or influence over the product. The partner commits scarce resources: staff time, data, integration effort, permissions, test environments, changes to workflow and the ability to influence the product.
